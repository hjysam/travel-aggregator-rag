EU261 Delay & Cancellation Rights (Simplified)
- Trains/Flights in the EU may provide compensation/refund depending on delay thresholds.
- Key thresholds (example, not exhaustive):
  * 60+ min delay: assistance (meals, refreshments) where applicable.
  * 120+ min delay or cancellation: rerouting or refund; compensation depends on distance/arrival delay.
- Always check the specific carrier policy for exact entitlements and exclusions.
